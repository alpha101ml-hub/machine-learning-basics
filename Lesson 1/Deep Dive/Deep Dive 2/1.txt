Deep Dive #2 - Vectorization vs Loops (with real timing)

Your Connected Exercise 1.1 already showed the speed difference. But why is it so dramatic?

- Python Loop: Each iteration checks types, increments counter, looks up the ist elements, performs addition, assigns back - all in Python interpreter (slow).

- NumPy vectorized: The entire operations is expressed in C loops (compiled) and often uses SIMD (single Intruction Multiple Data) - CPU processes 4-8 numbers in one clock cycle.
