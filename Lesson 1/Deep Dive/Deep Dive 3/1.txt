Deep Dive #3 - Enisum (demystified)

einsum looks like magic string incantation. But it's just a mini-language for tensor contractions.

The pattern: "input1_indices, input2_indices -> output_indices"

- Letters represent dimensions (any letters: i,j,k,...)
- Same letter in multiple inputs means multiply along that dinmension
- Letter missing from output means sum over that dimension
